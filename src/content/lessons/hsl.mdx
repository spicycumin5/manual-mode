## Edit one color at a time

**<Term id="hsl">HSL</Term>** (also called the Color Mixer) splits the image into color ranges (reds, oranges, yellows, greens, aquas, blues, purples, magentas) and lets you change each one's:

- **Hue**: shift the color itself (make greens more yellow, or blues more teal).
- **Saturation**: make that color more or less intense.
- **Luminance**: make that color brighter or darker.

## Recipes

| Goal | Adjustment |
|---|---|
| Deep, dramatic sky | Blue: luminance −30 to −50, saturation +20 |
| Natural skin | Orange: saturation −10, luminance +10 (skin is mostly orange) |
| Autumn pop | Orange & yellow: saturation +; yellow hue toward orange |
| Muted, "film" greens | Green: hue toward yellow, saturation −30 |
| Clean white snow | Blue & aqua: saturation − (removes blue cast in shadows) |

## Vibrance vs. saturation

**Saturation** boosts every color equally. **<Term id="vibrance">Vibrance</Term>** boosts muted colors more and leaves already-strong ones (and skin) mostly alone. Reach for vibrance first; it's harder to overdo.

<Mistakes>
- Cranking saturation globally. Skin turns orange and skies go neon. Target specific colors instead.
- Big hue shifts on skin tones. Small moves only.
</Mistakes>
