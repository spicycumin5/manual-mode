## Two sliders, total mood control

- **Temperature**: blue ↔ yellow. Fixes warm indoor light or cold shade.
- **Tint**: green ↔ magenta. Fixes fluorescent and some LED light, which add a green cast.

To neutralize: find something that *should* be neutral (white shirt, grey pavement, clouds) and adjust until it has no color. Many editors have an eyedropper that does this in one click.

## Correct vs. pleasing

"Correct" white balance isn't always the goal:

- **Sunsets and candlelight** look wrong when neutralized. Keep them warm.
- **Blue hour and snow** feel colder and more atmospheric slightly cool.
- **Skin** usually looks better a touch warm than a touch cool.

If you shot RAW, white balance changes are lossless. In JPEG, big shifts can make colors look muddy, so try to get it close in camera.
